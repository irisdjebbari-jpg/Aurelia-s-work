About the artist

Aurelia Voss is a contemporary mixed-media artist whose work dances between the tangible and the ethereal. Her canvases often blur the line between memory and dream, layering translucent washes of color over intricate graphite sketches. Each piece feels like a fragment of a forgotten story—half-whispered, half-imagined—inviting viewers to linger in the quiet spaces between brushstrokes. Born with an insatiable curiosity for textures, Aurelia experiments with unconventional materials: pressed flowers, fragments of handwritten letters, and even powdered stone. She describes her process as “listening to the silence between colors,” allowing intuition to guide her hand rather than rigid plans. Her exhibitions are less like galleries and more like immersive worlds—dimly lit rooms where paintings seem to breathe, and soft ambient sounds echo the mood of her work. Whether you leave feeling comforted or unsettled, Aurelia's art ensures you never leave unchanged. You can order Aurelia's art work on Etsy.
